Frequently asked questions

Questions garage owners usually ask.

Does TrimTech AI replace my existing phone number?

It can be configured around the way your garage wants to handle calls. The final setup can be tailored so the AI receptionist fits your existing customer journey rather than forcing your business into a generic call-centre process.

Can it actually book appointments?

Yes. TrimTech AI can check live availability and create garage appointments in the connected calendar, using the services and booking rules configured for that business.

Does it work differently for each garage?

Yes. The platform is business-aware. Each garage can have its own name, greeting, services, pricing, hours, calendar, contact details and operational data while using the shared TrimTech AI platform.

Can it handle vehicle information?

The garage workflow can capture a registration and use connected vehicle data to give the business useful vehicle context as part of the booking process.

What happens after a booking is made?

The booking appears in the connected systems and the reminder workflow can automatically contact the customer at the appropriate time before their appointment.
