Add Staff Manager to your home screen. An internet connection is required for private information and every action.

iPhone

  1. Tap Open Staff Manager below in Safari to open your sign-in page or dashboard.
  2. Tap Share, then Add to Home Screen (you may need to scroll).
  3. If offered, enable Open as Web App, name it Staff Manager, then tap Add.
  4. Open Staff Manager from your home screen and sign in. You may need to sign in again after installing.

Android

  1. Tap Open Staff Manager below in Chrome to open your sign-in page or dashboard.
  2. Use Install app when available, or open the browser menu and choose Install app or Add to Home screen.
  3. Open Staff Manager from your home screen and sign in.

Menu labels vary by browser. If installation is unavailable, use Staff Manager in your browser. Remove and re-add an older Home Screen icon if it still opens app help.
