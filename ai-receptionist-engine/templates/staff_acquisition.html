Metric definitions

Landing requests
Recorded successful GET requests served by the marketing application at /staff-manager.
Trial-click requests
Recorded GET requests to the marketing Start free trial redirect. Repeated requests, bots and link previews can increase both request metrics. Requests and clicks measure HTTP activity.
Registrations
Self-service workspaces with an existing trial record, counted by the business creation timestamp. Provisioned legacy workspaces are excluded.
Email verifications
Administrators with an eligible self-service workspace, counted once on their first stored verified_at timestamp.
Trial activations
Eligible trials counted on their stored starts_at timestamp. Expired trials remain eligible.
Account exclusions
Currently archived workspaces, accounts explicitly marked as tests, and inactive accounts discarded through registration review are excluded from all three account metrics. Restore or reclassification can change historical summaries. Test status is assigned manually through Manage business.
History and attribution
Existing account timestamps supply historical account totals. Request collection begins when persistent counters first record successfully. Earlier dates show Unavailable, and the first collection day is partial. Stored totals can undercount during tracking outages. Request counters retain no account identity, so known test requests cannot be removed retrospectively. Account events have no ad attribution in this report.
