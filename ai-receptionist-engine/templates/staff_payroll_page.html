Employee payroll settings

Use the employee’s HMRC-issued tax code and NI category. Opening balances must include any earlier payroll this tax year, including pay processed outside TrimTech. Pension rates are gross contribution percentages; relief at source deducts 80% of the employee contribution. No automatic enrolment assessment is performed.

This release supports regular weekly/monthly employee earnings only. Directors, salary sacrifice, student/postgraduate loans, attachment orders, payrolled benefits, statutory payments, irregular pay and corrections to finalized payroll require external payroll review.
